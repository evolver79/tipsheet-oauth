Tipsheet privacy policy

Tipsheet is a personal tool with a single user: its owner.

  • What it accesses: the list of YouTube channels the signed-in account subscribes to, through the YouTube Data API with the read-only scope (youtube.readonly).
  • What it stores: channel names, IDs and descriptions, on the owner's own home server.
  • What it never does: change anything on YouTube, read email or other Google data, or share, sell or send any data to third parties.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token stops working immediately.
